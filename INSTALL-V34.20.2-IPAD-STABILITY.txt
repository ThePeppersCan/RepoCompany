Dragonbound V34.20.2 — iPad Stability + Touch Hotfix

Install over V34.20.1 and replace matching files.

- Removes the normal-home virtual D-pad that was physically overlapping Dragon Racing / dragon taps on iPad.

Desktop behaviour, saves, races, story, economy, dragon AI and Career Mode logic are unchanged.
