DRAGONBOUND V34.19 — MOBILE / IPAD COMPATIBILITY PATCH
======================================================

Install over the current V34.18.3.3 build.
Copy this patch into the website root and replace matching files.

This is an ADDITIVE compatibility layer. It does not rebalance races, alter
story/save data, replace dragon animations, or redesign the desktop game.

WHAT IT DOES
------------
• Fits the complete Dragonbound 16:9 stage inside iPad/iPhone Safari's visible viewport.
• Adds iOS safe-area support (home indicator / rounded screen insets).
• Removes Career Mode's 520px minimum-height problem on short landscape screens.
• Keeps dialogue, choices, journals, calendar, travel, outings and modal actions scrollable/on-screen.
• Keeps the Home Calendar, Go Out and Career Mode launchers inside the tablet viewport.
• Improves touch targets without changing mapped invisible art hotspots.
• Makes furniture/build interactions touch-safe.
• Adds touch movement + ACT/RUN controls for compatible Dragonbound exploration/outing gameplay.
• Adds dedicated CREEP/RUN/ACT touch controls for Blackglass After Hours.
• Touch controllers appear only when gameplay needs them; desktop keyboard controls are unchanged.
• Adds a small landscape recommendation in portrait mode; portrait remains usable.
• Adds Meet the Teams tablet/touch fit rules.
