DRAGONBOUND CAREER MODE V34.28
THE MEASURE OF A LAP

V34.28 makes Practice & Qualifying a real technical sporting phase rather than another sequence of dialogue scenes.

PRACTICE
- The player selects the first-run learning objective.
- Practice produces real lap and sector data.
- The player is compared against the full grid and Tyrese benchmark.
- Setup is adjustable across Front Response, Stability, Endurance Bias and Qualifying Trim.
- Each axis has advantages and costs; there is no universal max-everything solution.
- Upper Terrace instability creates a telemetry diagnosis problem.
- Correct diagnosis improves setup confidence and technical progression; a wrong diagnosis is recoverable but less efficient.
- Final practice validates the changes before qualifying.

QUALIFYING
- Three-run structure: Bank One, Push, Final Run.
- Player selects a release strategy after Run One.
- Track evolution, traffic/tow risk, pressure handling, setup and Career Evolution all influence pace.
- Qualifying laps reveal sectors live before the final result appears.
- Positions are not simply assigned in advance.
- Lumerre is tuned as the point where the developing Quickquill racer can naturally become a 2nd–4th threat, while pole is possible after a particularly strong weekend.
- The final grid is saved for the upcoming Lumerre Crown race.

CAREER CONSEQUENCES
- Best qualifying is recorded.
- First career pole can be recorded permanently.
- Outqualifying Tyrese changes professional respect / competitive tension.
- Nearby grid rivals gain qualifying-battle history.
- Fame, media pressure, technical understanding and pressure handling can move from the result.
