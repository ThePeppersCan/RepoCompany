Repo Sports / Dragonbound V32.78 — Keeper House Visits

- Added “Visit Other Keepers” to the Dragonbound Travel menu.
- Added a searchable keeper-home browser showing keeper name, dragon name/breed and furniture count.
- Visiting a keeper loads their actual starter house and their saved furniture placements at the exact saved x/y/scale/direction values.
- Added a read-only public-house preview API with authenticated-only access; no visitor can edit another player's house or dragon data.
- Visitor dragons are live local previews: they use the real baby-dragon animation set and walk around independently in the owner's house while avoiding placed furniture.
- Visiting pauses your own home's dragon simulation until you return, so your own home state is not accidentally changed.
- Added “Other homes” and “Return Home” controls while visiting.
- All V32.77 layering, bath, furniture and starter-house fixes are preserved.
