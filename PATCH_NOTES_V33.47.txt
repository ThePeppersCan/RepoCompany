Velmora patch V33.47 — Velmora City Circuit playable V1

Implemented the first playable Dragon Racing course using the supplied Velmora City Circuit artwork.

Included:
- Track Select ENTER RACE control and compact race confirmation.
- Velmora City Circuit only launches; later courses remain preview/select only.
- Fullscreen camera-follow race world using the supplied circuit artwork.
- Manually defined closed centreline following the pale city roadway.
- 15 ordered checkpoints, 3 laps and 6 racers (player + 5 AI).
- Player uses their current Dragonbound dragon breed/name; AI uses existing baby-dragon assets.
- W/Up accelerate, S/Down brake, A/D lane steering and Space boost.
- Smooth course-relative movement, lateral overtaking, AI line changes and light separation.
- Compact race HUD: position, lap, time and boost.
- Countdown, lap/final-lap notices, results, race again, track select and leave flow.
- Per-dragon/account City Circuit best time, best lap, races, wins and podium stats.
- Dragon Racing progression record safely initialises at Level 1.
- Existing training levels provide only small race modifiers.
- Admin-only DragonRacingDebug path/checkpoint/racer tools.
- Menu music fades out into the race and resumes on Track Select.

IMPORTANT: Dragon Racing XP is intentionally DISABLED in this build. Finishing a race records times/results only and grants no XP.
