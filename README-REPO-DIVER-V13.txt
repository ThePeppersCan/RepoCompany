MAJOR V13 SYSTEMS
- Ecosystem-zone aware fish selection and spawning.
- Five expedition personalities: Calm Research Dive, Predator Waters, Migration Day, Salvage Opportunity, Strange Silence.
- Depth/habitat-linked populations rather than uniform random fish distribution.
- School cohesion/alignment/separation and coordinated scatter behaviour.
- Predator/prey hunting that is atmospheric only and never creates catch rewards.
- Wildlife awareness/noise response; boosting, sonar and harpoon shots disturb nearby animals.
- Sonar can scatter sensitive fish and attract curious/research species.
- Territorial warning behaviour and ambush/curious/protected wildlife states.
- Protected and juvenile research-only specimens.
- Server-persistent ecology observations from research photography.
- Research Dart tagging (R) with persistent tags and rare returning tagged animals.
- Selective release of the most recent catch (X) for six seconds after landing it.
- Lightweight within-expedition conservation: repeatedly taking the same species reduces only its later spawn preference for that same expedition.
- Living Ocean Expedition Director with deliberate quiet periods and anti-repeat event memory.
- Dynamic events: passing schools, predator hunts, current shifts, whale-song contacts, bioluminescent blooms, exposed salvage, distant mega-fauna, migration surges and sediment clouds.
- Visible current streaks, current-riding oxygen benefit, disturbed seabed sediment and distant mega-fauna silhouettes.
- 18 biome-specific three-zone ecology maps/names.
- Ecology Archive in Marine Journal and Living Ocean research section in Marine Institute.
- Field tag ledger with MR-### IDs.
- Existing Shared Ocean seasons/migrations continue to physically affect live expeditions.
- Migration expeditions influence Fish House demand for schooling-fish dishes.
- F8 developer ecology overlay (normal players never need it).
- Existing V10.1/V11 clean-audio rule retained: no continuous broadband/white-noise ambience.
