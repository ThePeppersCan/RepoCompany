REPO SPORTS V2 TEST 34 + WORLD CUP SHOOTOUT / REFEREE PATCH

4:30 REPO SPORTS V2 — DRAWS
- A 4:30 draw no longer goes straight to a drawn full-time screen.
- It now enters a penalty shootout.
- Three penalties per side.
- If still level after three each, sudden death continues until one side leads
  after an equal number of attempts.
- The first team to shoot is RANDOMISED 50/50.
- The first three shootout kicks rotate through all three team players instead
  of the same attacker taking every penalty.
- Full-time screen shows the regulation score and penalty score.
- Prediction payout now resolves against the shootout winner, so every standard
  rotation has a winner.

WORLD CUP
- World Cup already had a draw -> shootout trigger; this patch keeps it active
  and hardens the actual shootout.
- First penalty taker is now randomised 50/50 rather than always the left team.
- All three players rotate through the first three penalties.
- Sudden death remains active if level.
- Centre scoreboard shows live penalty score during the shootout.

REPO SPORTS LOGOS DURING V2 GOALS
- Removed the old V2 drawTournamentEnvironment image renderer.
- In V2, its 'team flags' are Repo Sports logo assets, so camera shake could
  visually read as Repo Sports logos being thrown around the pitch.
- V2 now draws NONE of those image objects on the field.
- Goal debris remains procedural only:
  flowers / petals
  paper confetti
  supporter scarves
- No logo/flag image is used as celebration debris.

WILLIAM WHISTLEWORTH
- Applied to BOTH World Cup and 4:30 V2.
- During ordinary open play William now trails the active play by ~0.115 pitch
  units instead of ~0.055.
- He also sits diagonally above/below the play by ~0.115 instead of hovering
  directly inside the player pack.
- Direction mirrors with possession so neither left nor right is favoured.
- Set pieces / fouls / VAR can still bring him closer when officiating requires it.
