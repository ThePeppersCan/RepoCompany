Velmora Dragon Racing — Patch V33.55
===================================

This patch only includes the modified files.

Changes
-------
1) Dramatically upgraded results screen.
   - Cleaner, more premium end-of-race presentation.
   - Better hierarchy, nicer stat cards, improved final standings board.
   - Player row highlighted more cleanly.

2) Race music no longer cuts out when results appear.
   - Countdown / transient race sounds stop.
   - Main race music and crowd continue on the finish screen.

3) Opponent variety widened.
   - The race now builds from the full available baby-dragon registry where possible.
   - Recent racers are avoided so you do not keep seeing the same 5 over and over.
   - Existing recurring named rivals are still kept in the pool.

4) Crowd atmosphere improved.
   - Extra confetti / celebratory bursts around trackside crowd areas.
   - Scene feels busier and more event-like.
