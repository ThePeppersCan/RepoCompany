Velmora: Dragonbound — V34.17 BLACKGLASS / CHAPTER FOUR

INSTALL
Copy the contents of this patch into the same website root used by V34.16 and replace matching files.
Preserve the included folder structure.

CHANGED FILES
- index.html
- dragon-racing-race.js
- dragon-racing-race.css
- dragonbound-career-launcher.js
- dragon-racing-assets/blackglass-night-circuit.webp
- dragonbound-career-mode/index.html
- dragonbound-career-mode/career.js
- dragonbound-career-mode/career.css

WHAT THIS VERSION DOES
1. Removes the brown / bronze broadcast UI treatment introduced by V34.16.
   Race Director panels, starting grid, battle graphics, final-lap graphics,
   results, reward panels and action buttons now use a cool navy / slate /
   cyan / silver broadcast palette.

2. Adds Quickquill Chapter Four: SECOND WIND / BLACKGLASS.
   Q18 The Briefing
   Q19 What Nobody Says
   Q20 The North Road
   Q21 Under the Floodlights
   Q22 Night Qualifying
   Q23 Second Wind — Blackglass race
   Q24 After the Storm

3. Adds a persistent one-lap Blackglass qualifying decision and six-racer grid.
   The resulting grid position carries into the story race.

4. Adds Blackglass Night Circuit as a real autonomous story race with named
   circuit sections, rain/floodlight atmosphere and Career race-memory data.

5. Restores Career story-race routing that was accidentally lost in V34.16.
   Canto and Blackglass story races return their result to Career Mode and do
   not use normal Dragon Racing rewards/records.

NOT CHANGED
- The restrained V34.16 Race Director camera philosophy remains: normal race
  zoom stays nearly fixed and does not bounce in/out repeatedly.
- Normal Dragonbound walking / takeoff / flight / landing sprites are untouched.
- NighLight spelling support is untouched.
- Quickquill private-quarters music files are not duplicated in this patch.

DEPENDENCY
This patch is intended to be installed over the current site that already has
V34.14.x Quickquill downtime/story assets and story-racer sprites.
