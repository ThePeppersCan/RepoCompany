BUG FIXED
V34.29.5 used equal progress per Catmull-Rom sample. The Lumerre route intentionally has many close-spaced waypoints in corners and very wide spacing on the long straights. Because pointAt() indexed those samples directly, the exact same race speed translated to enormous pixel movement on sparse straights and tiny pixel movement in dense corners. That is why the dragons looked like they teleported down straights and nearly stopped at every bend.

FIX
- Lumerre now uses arc-length progress.
- 1% of race progress always maps to 1% of the physical visible route length.
- Corner speed reduction is now the intended small racing slowdown only; no geometry-induced stopping.
- Acceleration / deceleration / overtaking / AI / camera remain owned by the existing Dragon Racing engine.
- City Circuit, Canto and Blackglass continue using their established progress mapping unchanged.

QA

- New 95th/5th percentile physical-speed ratio: ~1.00x.
- Lumerre only: arcLengthProgress flag gates the new behaviour.
