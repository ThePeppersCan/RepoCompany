LIVE PLAYER CAREER RECORDS
- Historical Repo Sports career totals remain the base.
- Each completed new live match adds:
  - +1 match for all six participating players
  - +1 win/loss according to their side
  - every regulation goal scored by that player
- Player win rate is recalculated from total wins / total matches.
- The left Top Goalscorers and Best Pet Win Rate boards refresh from live data.

MATCH RECORDING SAFETY
- Only the current global host lease can complete a match.
- Match serial is recorded server-side.
- Duplicate final-whistle messages cannot double-count the same result.
- Penalty shootout decides the win/loss, but shootout penalties are NOT added
  to Goals For / Goals Against or player career goals.

PREDICTIONS
- The +1,000 GP prediction claim is no longer admin-only.
- Normal signed-in users can use the live prediction feature.
