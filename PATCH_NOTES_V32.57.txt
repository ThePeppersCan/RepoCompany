V32.57 — Dragonbound Hestholm Room Surface Hotfix

This replaces the V32.56 Hestholm placement geometry rather than layering another tiny adjustment on top.

Fixes
- Downstairs buildable floor now extends lower toward the visible front edge of the wooden floor.
- Upstairs buildable floor is widened across the full right-hand landing.
- Removed the upstairs stair exclusion that was causing the preview to snap/jitter around the top of the stairs.
- Upstairs and downstairs pointer hit areas now have a hard gap at the stair lip, so the room detector cannot rapidly flip between floors.
- Hestholm upstairs no longer runs the automatic stair-exclusion nudge at all.
- The downstairs stair exclusion now ends above the actual buildable floor, so it does not steal valid floor space.
- Existing furniture/inventory/database placements are untouched.

Changed files only
- index.html
- dragonbound-furniture.js
- PATCH_NOTES_V32.57.txt

No Supabase migration required.
