REPO COMPANY V18.1 — BINDER POLISH V7
=====================================

Changes in this pass:
- Hidden Cards holder is significantly larger and remains beside the binder.
- Clicking the Hidden Cards holder plays the supplied card-shuffle sound at 60% volume.
- Cards are clipped/contained inside the real binder pockets so tall cards do not spill over pocket edges.
- Patch/tall cards get a slightly safer fit while ordinary cards still fill the pockets cleanly.
- White outer margins were removed from the affected patch-card PNG assets without changing the card artwork itself.
- Binder effects are alpha-masked to the actual transparent binder artwork, removing the harsh square/rectangle underneath the binder.
- The few effects that used full rectangular colour washes were softened to radial light only.

INSTALL
-------
1. Replace script.js with the included script.js.
2. Merge the included assets folder into the existing site assets folder.
   - This adds the Hidden Cards shuffle sound.
   - This replaces only the affected patch-card PNGs with cleaned versions.
   - Existing binder artwork/holder assets are also retained in the package.

No SQL/database changes are required.
