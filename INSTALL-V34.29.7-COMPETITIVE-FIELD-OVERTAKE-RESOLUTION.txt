DRAGONBOUND V34.29.7 — COMPETITIVE FIELD & OVERTAKE RESOLUTION

BASE
- Install over V34.29.6.
- Replace the root dragon-racing-race.js.
- Hard-refresh after copying.
- No database migration.
- No save reset.

WHY THIS PATCH EXISTS
V34.29.6 fixed physical movement around the Lumerre circuit, but the competitive model still had two problems:
1. Generic ±3.2% race luck plus Lumerre-only player bonuses could make September untouchable from the opening laps.
2. Overtake battles could repeatedly trigger without ever resolving into an actual pass or a defender-created gap.

V34.29.7 FIXES
- Lumerre-only race luck is compressed to a much tighter competitive spread.
- Removes the old free +0.16% Lumerre player pace bonus and reduces stacked training pace influence.
- Career AI pace now matters more than generic random luck.
- Tyrese, Jalen, Sofia, Luka, Ren and Maya can all have changing pace phases during the ten-lap race.
- Stronger Lumerre slipstream effect helps the following pack stay connected.
- Late-race stamina begins to distinguish racers naturally.
- Overtake attempts now resolve decisively after the side-by-side phase.
- A successful attacker gets a temporary clearing burst that physically completes the pass.
- A successful defender forces the attacker to reset and opens a real gap.
- Per-opponent cooldown prevents Tyrese/September from immediately repeating the same battle dozens of times.
- Cooldown is shared by both racers in the pair.
- Existing Canto, Blackglass and normal Dragon Racing balance is unchanged; these balance changes are Lumerre story-race specific.

TARGET FEEL
- A strong September can win, but pole/front-row is not protection.
- Tyrese can genuinely pass September.
- Other racers can remain attached to the fight and gain positions.
- Battles end as PASS or DEFENDED rather than infinite repeated challenges.
- Normal race target: several genuine field overtakes, not dozens of non-events.
