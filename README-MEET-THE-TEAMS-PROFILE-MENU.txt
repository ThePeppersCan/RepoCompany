REPO SPORTS — MEET THE TEAMS PLAYER PROFILE MENU

Changes:
- Meet the Teams no longer opens the family tree image.
- It now opens the supplied MENU.png artwork as a dedicated full-screen team/player selection screen.
- All 48 player names have invisible interactive hit areas aligned over the artwork.
- Player buttons are deliberately non-functional for now; each is pre-wired with data-country and data-player values for the future profile viewer.
- Enter/Space keyboard activation is also ready.
- Opening Meet the Teams pauses the normal World Cup menu track and starts the supplied Sports Champions PS3 OST at 30% volume.
- Closing the screen stops the Meet the Teams track and resumes the normal World Cup menu music from where it was paused.
- Escape, the close X, and clicking the dark backdrop return to the World Cup hub.
- Existing World Cup/TCG/Rooftops/Barry systems are otherwise untouched.
