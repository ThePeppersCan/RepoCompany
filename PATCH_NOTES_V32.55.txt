V32.55 — Dragonbound Furniture Room Placement + Transparency QA

PLACEMENT FIXES
- Reworked furniture targeting so the player no longer has to hit a razor-thin strip of floor pixels.
- The cursor may now move anywhere inside the visible upstairs/downstairs room interior; furniture is projected down onto that room's authored wooden floor band.
- Hestholm Fjord starter upstairs placement was widened to include the right-hand side of the room.
- Hestholm Fjord starter downstairs floor band was corrected and widened so the full lower room can be furnished.
- Roof/garden/outside regions remain invalid.
- The room-selection logic handles the small overlap between room hit areas by choosing the nearest actual floor.
- Edge projection now accounts for the furnishing footprint/scale so snapping to a room edge does not immediately fail validation.
- New furniture now starts at 70% scale by default; existing saved sizes are preserved and can still be resized 55%–160%.
- Build remains OWNED FURNITURE ONLY. Furniture can still only be bought/claimed through Bonnie's Adoption Centre shop.

FURNITURE ART QA
- Corrected transparency on 17 supplied furniture sprites.
- Removed large white/near-white generated background holes from canopy beds, tables/benches, cart furniture, rope training pieces and other affected items.
- Removed several detached generation fragments sitting at the far edges of affected PNGs.
- Preserved intentional pale/white object details such as pillows, towels, porcelain and the training mirror surface.
