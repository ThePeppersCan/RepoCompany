ADVANCED BUILD PROMPT — DRAGONBOUND V34.31
CHAPTER EIGHT: THE FIRST FULL SEASON — OPENING WEEK

MISSION
Build the first part of Chapter Eight for Velmora: Dragonbound Career Mode.
This is the moment the game stops feeling like a chain of authored assessment
chapters and becomes a real career framework. It must remain simple and clear
like FIFA Career Mode, but the player must regularly do something more
interesting than click through dialogue or watch cards appear.

The player does not directly fly the dragon during races. Race weekends remain
Watch, Quick Sim or Sim. Interactive mechanics happen around the racing:
planning, investigation, strategy, team decisions, relationships and pressure.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES
- Premium Quickquill navy/slate/ivory interface. Never brown.
- Short instructions, large readable actions and immediate feedback.
- Every scene must use a different interaction grammar.
- Do not copy Blackglass After Hours, but match its sense of agency, discovery
  and consequences.
- No fake buttons. Every visible primary action must advance or change state.
- No random hidden failure. Results must be understandable from player input.
- Existing Verdict contract, Tyrese stance, Sunscale interest, relationships,
  racecraft and saved results must affect Chapter Eight.
- Preserve all old saves. No database migration.

CHAPTER FLOW — SIX DISTINCT MECHANICS

Q75 — THE CALENDAR OPENS
Interaction: explorable season route.
- Reveal an eight-round Velmora Championship calendar.
- Let the player inspect event cards in any order.
- Each venue shows character, risk, likely rival and why it matters.
- Require the opener and finale to be inspected.
- Player chooses a season ambition: build consistently, hunt podiums, or fight
  the front of the championship.

Q76 — SIX HOURS
Interaction: constrained weekly-planning board.
- Give the player six preparation hours.
- Activities have different costs and visible effects:
  Dragon Bond, Flight Lab, Strength, Engineering, Recovery and Sponsor Duty.
- Allocate exactly six hours, with a maximum of three in one activity.
- Contract sponsor-day reductions must be reflected.
- Show live Pace, Control, Stamina, Bond and Team-readiness meters.
- Locking the week creates a persistent preparation profile.

Q77 — THE MISSING TENTH
Interaction: telemetry deduction puzzle.
- A tenth of a second disappears only in crosswind conditions.
- Player inspects four evidence sources: player trace, Tyrese comparison, wind
  log and equipment calibration.
- Require three clues before accusing a cause.
- Choices must be technically plausible.
- Correct diagnosis rewards engineering trust and technical understanding.
- A wrong first diagnosis receives a useful Nell hint and allows one retry.
- Never hard fail the chapter.

Q78 — PIT WALL, LIVE
Interaction: multi-stage command drill.
- Present five changing race-control situations.
- Each has gauges, radio context and three commands.
- Calls include traffic, crosswind, overheating, rival vulnerability and a
  Quickquill team-order dilemma.
- Score each decision immediately and explain why.
- Carry Pace, Stamina, Dragon and Team integrity between stages.
- Final rank: CALM HAND, RACE READER or COMMAND VOICE.
- Tyrese relationship and player identity can alter the final team-order call.

Q79 — THE PROMISES YOU PICK
Interaction: objective draft.
- Choose exactly three objectives from six.
- Categories: Results, Teammate, Development, Reliability, Technical and Media.
- Each card shows reward and pressure.
- At least one Team objective and one Personal objective are required.
- Objectives persist into the season dashboard.
- No generic “complete ten races” filler.

Q80 — SEASON CONTROL
Interaction: persistent career dashboard.
- Show calendar, opening standings, chosen objectives, preparation profile,
  telemetry result and pit-wall grade in one professional control room.
- Let the player choose the default race-weekend presentation:
  Watch Live, Quick Sim or Full Sim.
- Make clear this sets a preference; it does not start a fake race.
- Unlock the reusable Season HQ and tease Round One: Velmora City Circuit.
