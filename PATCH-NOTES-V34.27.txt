CORE SYSTEMS
- Save-aware fame reactions on arrival/parade.
- Open-schedule paddock encounters instead of one fixed dialogue chain.
- Inbox and rumour hooks for future Career Mode chapters.
- Four-part Crown Challenge with persistent results.
- Head-to-head sprint uses Career Evolution race-battle resolution.
- Reception conversations are selected from the professional grid and affect persistent career relationships/reputation.
- Optional circuit-overlook moment can be discovered rather than automatically served.
- Crown Week progression is kept separate from the full Lumerre race-weekend completion state.
