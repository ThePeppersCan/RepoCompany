ROOM UNLOCK RULE
- Each live room opens exactly 10 minutes before its stated UK kickoff time.
- Times are locked to British Summer Time (+01:00) for August 2026.

  * date and kickoff time
  * live countdown until the room opens

- When the countdown reaches zero, ENTER LIVE ROOM becomes available.
