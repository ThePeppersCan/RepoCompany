V32.90 — Dragonbound Persistent Care + Gradual Furniture Needs

Persistence fix
- Fixed the remaining close/reopen needs + Bond reset path.
- Live care is now snapshotted to BOTH Dragonbound browser save records every 2 seconds while at home.
- Closing, hiding or reloading the page also performs an immediate synchronous local care save before the async Supabase sync.
- Profile hydration now compares both browser snapshots with Supabase and uses the newest timestamped copy.
- Slow/stale Supabase responses can no longer overwrite a newer local needs/Bond snapshot.
- Supabase is repaired from the newest browser snapshot after reopening when required.

Gradual furniture care
- Beds no longer restore Energy instantly. Dragons now need to remain asleep for the benefit to build gradually.
- Baths / sand baths / grooming now restore Hygiene gradually while the dragon actually uses them.
- Toys and play furniture restore Fun gradually over the complete play session.
- Feeding/drinking, rest furniture, training, scratch/dig/climb, warm spots and other usable furniture now also apply their care effects gradually.
- Interrupting an activity early grants only the portion of care actually completed.
- Proper naps are longer (roughly 18–32+ seconds depending on personality), baths roughly 12–20 seconds, and play sessions roughly 11–19+ seconds.
- Existing relaxed daily-care decay and gentle low-needs Bond loss from V32.89 are retained.
