Velmora Dragon Racing — Patch V33.57
===================================

Patch-only update.

What changed
------------
- Race camera now zooms in much more during active racing.
- Follow mode now holds a tighter shot on the main pack/player area.
- Close-battle, final-lap, final-straight, and photo-finish views are all more immersive.
- Wide zoom-outs happen less often and for shorter bursts.
- Pan-ahead moments are rarer so the race feels less pulsy and more locked-in.
- Camera tracking is a touch snappier and the maximum race zoom cap has been increased.

Result
------
The track should feel bigger, the dragons should feel closer, and the race should stay more immersive overall.
