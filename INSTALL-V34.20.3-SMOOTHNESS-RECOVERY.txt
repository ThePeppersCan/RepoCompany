DRAGONBOUND V34.20.3 — SMOOTHNESS RECOVERY

Install over V34.20.2 and replace matching files.

This is deliberately a recovery/stability pass, not another redesign.

KEPT
- V34.20 lazy/scene-based asset loading (no return to loading the whole game up front)

- V34.20.2 iPad safe-area, tap reliability, Dragon Racing and petting fixes

- Tiny interaction SFX are warmed so first taps feel immediate

NOT CHANGED

Goal: keep the bandwidth/RAM wins from V34.20 while restoring the responsive feel of the pre-optimisation build.
