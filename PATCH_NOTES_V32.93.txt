V32.93 — Physical Dragon Interactions, Skill Rewards & Journal Readability
22 August 2026

PHYSICAL DRAGON INTERACTIONS
- Added a generic physical-interaction layer driven by furniture tags, category, footprint and names across the existing 330-item furniture catalog.
- Small suitable toys can be visually carried by the dragon and follow its facing direction.
- Carry interactions use short controlled safe-floor routes rather than a full physics engine.
- Rollable items use bounded temporary movement/rotation and reset cleanly after use.
- Cupboards, storage and other suitable furnishings can visibly enter a temporary open state while investigated.
- Beds, baths and other mountable furnishings now have an approach/settle phase followed by a short eased movement onto the use position rather than an immediate snap.
- Bath audio/effects begin after the dragon has settled into the interaction.
- Added lightweight bath, fire, feeding, puzzle and training effects.
- Training equipment can use short safe micro-routes to make exercise look more physical.
- Physical state is temporary and safely cleaned when an interaction ends, Build Mode opens, Travel begins, another house is visited, the home closes, or the actor is destroyed.
- Gradual needs and gradual skill XP remain intact; gains wait until the settle phase is complete and interruption only retains progress earned so far.

PERSONALITY & FAVOURITES
- Physical interaction behaviour continues to react to traits such as Professional Napper, Splash Addict, Food Goblin, Greedy and Little Athlete.
- Added per-type favourite tracking for beds, toys, baths, training items and feeding stations without replacing the existing overall favourite furniture field.
- Added lightweight life tracking for carried objects, ball play, cupboard investigations, puzzle interactions and physical training sessions.
- Added occasional memories such as First Toy Carried and First Cupboard Investigation.

KEEPER MARK SKILL REWARDS
- Added balanced one-time Keeper Mark rewards for genuine skill milestones:
  Lv10 +5, Lv20 +7, Lv30 +10, Lv40 +12, Lv50 +15, Lv60 +18,
  Lv70 +22, Lv80 +28, Lv90 +40, Lv95 +50, Lv100 +75.
- Maximum from completely maxing one skill: 282 Keeper Marks.
- Maximum across all six completely maxed skills: 1,692 Keeper Marks.
- No Marks are awarded per XP tick or per furniture use.
- The journal shows the next Keeper Mark milestone on each skill card.
- Historical skill levels are baselined when V32.93 is first used so old progress does not dump retroactive currency into the economy.
- Rewards use secure server-side Supabase RPCs. The client never supplies the reward amount.
- Claims are transaction-safe/idempotent through a unique user/skill/milestone key and wallet row locking.
- Live backend migration has been deployed.

KEEPER JOURNAL READABILITY
- Reworked Keeper Journal typography based on the supplied screenshot.
- Increased tiny metadata labels, skill text, rank text, Life Stats labels, habit chips, memory dates/descriptions and helper text to readable desktop sizes.
- Slightly enlarged cards/spacing and allowed sensible wrapping rather than crushing text to 6–8px.
- Right-hand journal content continues to scroll naturally.
- Added responsive sizing for normal desktop/laptop layouts.

BABY AGE / GROWTH LOCK
- Dragons now remain baby-era dragons for much longer.
- Newly Hatched: first 3 real days.
- Baby: day 3 through day 90.
- Growing Baby: starts at day 90.
- Later Young Dragon/Juvenile definitions remain in code for future artwork, but V32.93 explicitly caps visible/gameplay growth at Growing Baby until later-life sprites are deliberately enabled.
- Real age still records correctly; the dragon simply does not age into unsupported artwork/stages.
- Visual growth changes remain extremely subtle and never swap away from the existing baby sprite set.

OTHER
- Existing 4× excited Dragon Bites chase speed is retained.
- Existing persistence, needs, Bond, growth/skills, personality, house visits, furniture shop/placement and petting systems are retained.
- No full physics engine or expensive per-frame furniture scan was added.
