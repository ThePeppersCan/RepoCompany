REPO SPORTS V2 TEST 15 — MORE SHOOTING / LESS PASS-DEFENCE LOOP

ROOT CAUSE FIXED
- passesSinceShot was incremented when a pass started.
- A successful pass then called setPossession().
- setPossession() reset passesSinceShot back to 0 and lastPasser to null.
- Result: the anti-pass-loop logic almost never saw a real multi-pass chain.
- It also meant the previous passer could be lost before a goal, hurting assists.
- Successful SAME-TEAM passes now preserve both the pass-chain count and the
  correct assist candidate.
- Real turnovers/restarts still clear them normally.

MORE ATTACKING ACTION
- Build-up, circulation, probing, counter and final-third phases all have more
  shooting intent and less passing weight.
- After just 1 completed pass in useful territory, shot weight starts rising.
- After 3+ passes, the attack becomes strongly encouraged to shoot or drive.
- Long-range shot intent is no longer crushed to a fixed 0.008.
- Actual shots are allowed from zone 0.44 rather than waiting for zone 0.58.
- Medium-range attempts remain harder to score because the existing distance
  and zone penalties still apply.
- Big-chance wind-up shortened from roughly 0.88–1.50s to roughly 0.68–1.08s.
- Decisions after passes and drives happen sooner.

LESS CONSTANT DEFENSIVE INTERRUPTION
- Pass interception read window reduced.
- Pass interception probability reduced and capped at 50%.
- Carrier tackle radius reduced.
- Tackle attempt probability reduced and capped at 50%.
- Successful tackle probability reduced slightly.
- Tackles/interceptions remain a real part of play, but should no longer break
  up nearly every developing attack.

SCORING / FAIRNESS
- Goal probability per shot was NOT increased in Test 15.
- The extra scoring should come from creating more actual attempts.
- Both teams use exactly the same action weights, tackle/interception rules,
  shot eligibility and goal RNG.
- No favourites.
- No rubber-banding.
- No comeback logic.
- No target score.
- Results remain capable of being cagey or wild purely through symmetric RNG.
