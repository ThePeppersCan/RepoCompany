V32.68 — Dragonbound Baby-Dragon Grounding / Missing-Legs Hotfix

WHAT WAS WRONG
- Baby dragon sprites were being anchored at only 90% of their image height.
- The prepared sprite frames actually carry their feet/body pixels to roughly 98–100% of the image height.
- That meant the bottom ~8–10% of many stationary sprites was being pushed below the authored floor position, making feet/legs look cut off or missing.
- Calvora made the issue especially obvious, but the grounding bug affected the shared renderer and could show on multiple breeds.

FIX
- The shared baby-dragon renderer now grounds every pose at 98.5% of the sprite image height.
- Idle/look poses now keep their feet above the floor instead of sinking into it.
- The same correction applies consistently to sitting, resting, sleeping, walking and furniture-interaction poses without changing movement/pathfinding.
- No breed-specific art has been replaced; the supplied transparent frames remain intact.

QA
- Inspected all 30 breeds across their first 8 semantic frames (240 frames).
- Alpha-bounds check confirms visible pixels reach ~97.6–100% of frame height, validating the previous 90% anchor as too low.
- JavaScript syntax check passed after the renderer change.
- Existing V32.67 binder favourite fix and V32.66 Dragonbound/furniture/Marks changes are preserved.
