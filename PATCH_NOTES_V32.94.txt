V32.94 — Furniture Mount Fix + Time-Paced Skill Training

BED / BATH PLACEMENT FIX
- Fixed the V32.93 physical-interaction cleanup regression that cleared the newly calculated furniture mount target before the mount animation began.
- Dragons now preserve the target for beds, baths, sand baths, perches, rest surfaces, hideaways and other mounted interactions.
- Bed/bath use once again moves the dragon onto/into the furnishing before the active interaction continues.
- Existing gradual Energy/Hygiene gains remain unchanged: leaving early only keeps progress earned so far.

SKILL TRAINING BALANCE
- Added persistent PER-SKILL training cooldowns. Switching to another training furnishing cannot bypass them.
- Cooldowns increase gently with skill level so high-level skills are long-term achievements:
  Lv 0–9: 10 min
  Lv 10–24: 15 min
  Lv 25–44: 20 min
  Lv 45–64: 30 min
  Lv 65–79: 45 min
  Lv 80–89: 60 min
  Lv 90–100: 90 min
- A real training session still awards XP gradually throughout the session; the cooldown begins after meaningful XP was earned.
- Dragons can still physically use/train on equipment while a skill is recovering, but that skill will not award more XP until ready.
- Cooldowns are stored inside dragon_memory, so refresh/reload cannot reset them.
- Existing same-furniture fatigue remains as an additional anti-spam layer.

PASSIVE XP ANTI-SPAM
- Indoor-flight experience: small award, max once per 4 minutes.
- Zoomies Agility experience: small award, max once per 6 minutes.
- Stair Agility/Confidence experience: max once per 8 minutes.
- Confidence from genuinely new locations remains one-time per discovered location.

JOURNAL
- Each skill now shows whether it is Ready to train or its current Recovery time.
- Keeper Mark milestones remain unchanged and therefore inherit the slower, fairer skill pacing.

FUTURE RACING
- Skills remain permanent, persistent 0–100 values and are deliberately paced as long-term dragon development, ready to become inputs to the future Dragon Flying/Racing system.

GROWTH
- V32.93 baby-art lock remains: dragons stay in baby-era growth art and cannot progress beyond Growing Baby until later sprites are intentionally enabled.

DATABASE
- No new SQL is required for V32.94.
