WATCH PARTY FLASHING FIX
ROOT CAUSE:
- Every 2.5-second Watch Party request sent a CatAsthma-only local payload FIRST.
- The real server payload containing the other active viewers arrived afterwards.
- This made other Watchcards literally disappear and reappear on every poll.

FIX:
- Instant local-only payload is now sent ONLY on initial iframe load.
- Normal refreshes send the real viewer payload without shrinking to one viewer first.
- Added a 12-second recent-viewer grace cache inside V2.
- A single missed/incomplete heartbeat will not make a viewer blink out.
- Genuine inactive viewers disappear after the grace period.
- Watchcard backgrounds and hover profiles are unchanged.
