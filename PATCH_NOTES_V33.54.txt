Velmora Dragon Racing — Patch V33.54
===================================

What changed
------------
1) Camera behaviour refined.
   - Reduced the constant zoom in / zoom out feeling.
   - Default race camera now stays closer and follows the pack/player more often.
   - Random broadcast cuts happen less often.
   - Wide shots are rarer and less dramatic.
   - Zoom easing is smoother so transitions feel less twitchy.

2) Race atmosphere made more alive.
   - Added subtle sun / light glow to the scene.
   - Added floating sparkle motes.
   - Added drifting colourful flying flags / pennants.
   - Added intermittent confetti style ambience.

Files included
--------------
- dragon-racing-race.js
- dragon-racing-race.css
- PATCH_NOTES_V33.54.txt

Apply by replacing the matching files in your current Dragon Racing build.
