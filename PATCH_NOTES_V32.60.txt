DRAGON INTERACTIONS
The furniture system now supplies richer semantic tags and the personality engine routes them into appropriate behaviours.

Examples:
- Beds / hammocks / bunks: sleep
- Sofas / cushions / perches: rest or perch
- Toys / tug posts / chew toys / hoards: play
- Food bowls / feeding tables: eat
- Water troughs / hydration stations: drink
- Baths / sand pits: wash / sand-bathe
- Grooming posts / polishing stations: groom
- Exercise wheels / treadmills / balance gear / gym equipment: train
- Roaring targets and horns: practise roaring
- Flame brazier: practise tiny flames
- Scratching logs / pillars: scratch
- Digging patch: dig
- Ramps / trellis / climbing panel: climb
- Hideaways: hide/rest
- Warm lamps / fireplace guard: bask
- Curtains: window-watch
- Bookshelves: investigate/read
- Mirrors: inspect reflection
- Herb planter: sniff
- Litter station: use the litter station

These interactions are weighted by each dragon's permanent personality, needs and quirks, so different dragons favour different furniture.

WALL FURNITURE
Wall décor is now treated separately from floor furniture:
- paintings, wall art, clocks, curtains, growth chart, towel rail, wall display, trophy shelf, wall medicine cabinet, etc. can mount on the room wall
- wall-mounted items do NOT create fake floor collision boxes
- dragons can still walk over and inspect appropriate wall objects
- floor furniture still snaps to the authored wooden floor surfaces
