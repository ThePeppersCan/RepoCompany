REPO DIVER V10.1 — CLEAN AMBIENCE HOTFIX

Fixes the constant white-noise / hiss character of V10 ambient sound.

Changes:
- Removed continuously looping noise generators from dive ambience.
- Removed continuous noise beds from harbour and Fish House ambience.
- Replaced them with quiet low-frequency modulated tonal layers.
- Reduced ambient bus gain from 0.62 to 0.34.
- Reworked restaurant ambience into sparse plate/glass tonal details.
- Reworked harbour ambience into occasional low wave swells rather than noise.
- Preserved short impact/noise transients for harpoon hits, boss stingers and other events where noise is useful.
- Cache version bumped so browsers fetch the new audio.js after deploy.

No gameplay, economy, Supabase or Repo Diver progression changes.
