Velmora Dragon Racing — Patch V33.63
===================================

Patch-only update.

Results overhaul
----------------
- Rebuilt the end-of-race screen as an official VRN classification broadcast.
- Keeps race music and crowd ambience alive underneath the result.
- Adds a short cinematic breathing window after the finish before the result appears.
- Player dragon, finishing position and result status now form the main hero moment.
- Adds Finish Time, Best Lap, Personal Best, Grid and Positions Gained/Lost.
- Final classification shows winner's full time and gaps-to-winner for the rest of the field.
- Adds factual Race Notes generated from the actual race result.
- Adds genuine First Win / First Podium / Personal Best / Best Lap badges only when earned.
- Keeps Dragon Racing XP disabled.

Live race UI cleanup
--------------------
- Nameplates reduced to slim, low-profile broadcast identifiers.
- Live leaderboard reduced substantially in width/height and visual weight.
- Overtake row movement remains smooth, but the panel is much less intrusive.
