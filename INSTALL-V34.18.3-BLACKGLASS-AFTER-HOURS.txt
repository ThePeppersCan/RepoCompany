WHAT'S NEW
- Q26 is now Blackglass: After Hours, inserted between the post-qualifying night and race morning.
- Direct control of the player's dragon with WASD.
- SHIFT creep / SPACE run / E interact.
- Normal movement, noise, hiding and Garran patrol/hearing gameplay.
- Pantry snack objective, old timing-sheet study, dropped pass, service key and hidden viewing rail.
- Getting caught is a story outcome, not a game-over.
- After Hours decisions feed into dragon state, Steward respect/reputation and the Blackglass race.
- Midnight timing sheets can grant one extra real sector-knowledge bonus in the race engine.
- Full supplied sound set integrated at restrained volumes; long clips were trimmed where useful.
- Blackglass Career race music from V34.18.2 remains untouched at 40%.

SAVE COMPATIBILITY
- Existing saves are retained.
- Saves at Q27/Q28 which have not completed Blackglass are gently moved to the new After Hours interlude once.
- Saves already in the Blackglass race or beyond are not rewound.
