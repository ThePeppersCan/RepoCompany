V32.92 — Dragon Growth, Skills & Training Progression

NEW: SIX PERMANENT DRAGON SKILLS
- Flying
- Agility
- Strength
- Fire Control
- Intelligence
- Confidence
- Skills use persistent XP, a visible 0–100 level and readable ranks:
  Untrained → Beginner → Developing → Capable → Skilled → Talented → Exceptional → Masterful.
- Higher levels require increasingly more XP. Skills never decay.

ORGANIC / GRADUAL TRAINING
- Skill XP is earned continuously while the dragon is actually performing the activity.
- Interrupting a session early only keeps the XP earned during the elapsed time.
- Beds, baths, toys and feeding retain V32.90's gradual need-restoration model.
- Sleeping/bathing do not grant unrelated skill XP.
- Normal indoor flight gives tiny Flying + Confidence experience.
- Zoomies give a tiny Agility gain.
- Stairs give an extremely small Agility / Confidence gain.
- Exploring a genuinely new house location gives Confidence once; repeatedly walking the same route cannot farm it.

FURNITURE → SKILL MAPPING
- The complete furniture catalog is interpreted dynamically from tags, category and furnishing name.
- Flight/launch/landing equipment → Flying (+ some Agility).
- Balance, weave, climbing, hurdles, ramps, sprint and obstacle furniture → Agility.
- Weights, resistance, sleds, cables, lifting and punching equipment → Strength.
- Fire-practice furniture → Fire Control.
- Puzzles, sorting, navigation, learning boards and reading → Intelligence.
- New/challenging/noisy/training experiences can contribute small Confidence gains.
- Push Weight Platform is explicitly treated as training despite its legacy comfort/sleep tags.
- Recovery Stretch Nest remains primarily recovery furniture rather than a skill grinder.

PERSONALITY / NATURAL TALENT
- Personality core stats subtly modify learning speed.
- Little Athlete, Little Pilot, Explorer/Adventurous, Fearless, Coward, Couch Potato,
  Toy Obsessed, Furniture Inspector and Food Goblin can influence relevant learning.
- Learning modifiers are deliberately modest (normally ~0.80x–1.28x), so no dragon is locked out of a skill.

ANTI-SPAM TRAINING FATIGUE
- Repeating the exact same furnishing shortly after using it gives reduced SKILL XP only.
- Care restoration is never reduced by skill fatigue.
- Switching to a different training item avoids the same-item penalty.
- A proper sleep/rest session recovers training fatigue faster.

GROWTH STAGES
- Newly Hatched: day 0–2
- Baby: day 2–7
- Growing Baby: day 7–18
- Young Dragon: day 18–35
- Juvenile: day 35+
- Age is the dominant factor. Care/Bond only give a tiny progress nudge.
- No adult stage has been added.
- Growth applies subtle uniform sprite scaling only and does not change navigation/collision geometry.
- Later growth stages receive tiny movement confidence increases; Dragon Bites excited chase speed remains separate.
- Higher Flying skill slightly improves the size/smoothness of safe indoor flight routes while staying inside flight zones.

MY DRAGON JOURNAL
- Added a full Growth & Skills section.
- Shows current growth stage and progress toward the next stage.
- Shows all six skill levels, ranks and progress to the next rank.
- Natural aptitude flavour notes only appear after enough personality has been observed.
- Skill rank-ups, first flight, first puzzle, first fire practice, first training and growth changes feed into Little Memories.

PERSISTENCE / BACKWARD COMPATIBILITY
- Skills, XP, fatigue and growth metadata live inside the SAME dragon_memory behaviour snapshot as Needs and Bond.
- Existing local/newest-Supabase timestamp merge remains the single persistence strategy.
- No extra database table or SQL migration is required.
- Existing dragons get conservative starting skill XP from recorded life history, capped around early progression so old saves are not over-rewarded.
