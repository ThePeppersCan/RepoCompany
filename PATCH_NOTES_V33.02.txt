V33.02 — MY BEDROOM / HUMAN KEEPER ROOM
=========================================

PATCH TYPE
- Small overlay patch for V33.01+.
- This is NOT a full 60–70 MB project archive.
- Overlay these files onto the existing site.

WHAT'S NEW
- Added the supplied My Bedroom button to the Dragonbound home sidebar area.
- My Bedroom opens a completely separate human/keeper bedroom screen.
- Dragonbound dragons are deliberately excluded from this room and cannot use its furniture.
- Added all 4 supplied room designs, all FREE:
  * Nordic Timber
  * Lakeside Stone
  * Terracotta Artisan
  * Midnight Dragon
- Added a bedroom-only Free Furniture Shop containing all 36 supplied sprites.
- No Keeper Marks and no GP are charged.
- Furniture can be placed multiple times, selected, flipped, put away and directly dragged while Edit Room is active.
- Rugs render below furniture and do not block other pieces.
- Wall-style furniture is constrained to a safe strip near the back wall.
- Placement uses a hidden snap grid, floor bounds and simple footprint overlap rejection.
- Room selection and furniture placement auto-save.

FUTURE CAT / DOG PREPARATION
- The 4 supplied dragon_nest assets are exposed as Future Pet Care "Pet Nest" items.
- Feeding Station and Scratching Perch are also in Future Pet Care.
- They are decorative/reserved right now; no dragon enters the bedroom.
- Metadata is kept separate so a future bedroom cat/dog can use these without touching Dragonbound's dragon AI.

PERSISTENCE
- Local fallback key: velmoraBedroom:v1:<username>
- Live Supabase table: public.player_bedrooms
- Live RPCs:
  * get_my_bedroom()
  * save_my_bedroom(p_room_id, p_placements)
- Saved placement shape:
  { id, assetId, x, y, flipped, scale }
- Room save is completely separate from Dragonbound dragon memory/furniture/wallets.

LIVE BACKEND
