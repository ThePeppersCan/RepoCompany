STORY RACE RULES
- The selected strategy gives only a small race nudge; random race luck remains dominant.
- Story races do not award ordinary Dragon Racing Keeper Marks, GP or Racing XP.
- Story races do not alter ordinary circuit records or AI race stats.
- The result returns rank, finish time, best lap, grid movement, overtakes, lead changes and photo-finish status to Career Mode.
- Race Again / Track Select are replaced by Continue Story during the Canto story race.
- The Career save is marked in-progress before the race starts and can safely resume after an abort/refresh.
