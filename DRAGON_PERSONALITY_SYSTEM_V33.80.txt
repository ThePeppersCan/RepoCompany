DRAGONBOUND V33.80 — PERSONALITY SYSTEM REFERENCE

1. ARCHITECTURE
The expanded personality universe is stored per dragon and versioned. It contains stable seeded axes, innate/secondary traits, discovered traits, quirks/discovered quirks, observations, furniture relationships, dislikes, habits, recent activity, transition history and relationship metadata.

The engine is deliberately layered:
- core numeric tendencies create nuance;
- named traits make those tendencies readable to players;
- quirks are narrower recurring behaviours;
- habits emerge from repeated transitions/actions;
- favourites/dislikes come from lived furniture interactions;
- discovery determines what the keeper is allowed to know.

2. TRAIT UNIVERSE
V33.80 ships with 133 possible expanded traits. Families include:
- Energy
- Rhythm
- Social
- Keeper Bond
- Curiosity
- Play
- Food
- Training
- Sleep
- Cleanliness
- Emotion
- Courage
- Environment
- Routine

A dragon receives a small subset, not all traits. The same breed can therefore produce substantially different individuals.

3. QUIRKS
V33.80 ships with 51 possible quirks. Quirks are intentionally specific and smaller than traits: repeated bedtime routes, favourite-window behaviour, post-meal naps, private training preference and similar recognisable pet-like patterns.

Quirks remain hidden until enough relevant evidence exists.

4. PERSISTENCE / MIGRATION
Existing dragons are upgraded lazily. If a valid V33.80 personality universe is absent, one is generated once from a stable per-dragon seed and saved into the existing dragon behaviour memory structure.

Once generated, reload/travel/reopen uses the saved universe rather than rerolling it.

Existing legacy Dragonbound traits and observations are preserved and merged into player-facing discovery where appropriate.

5. DISCOVERY
The player is not shown the complete generated personality.

Observed evidence is built from real behaviour counters and transitions. Innate/core traits normally require less evidence than secondary traits. The engine also rate-limits discovery so several traits cannot dump onto the screen at once.

The journal therefore moves from uncertainty -> observation -> confirmed nature over time.

6. BEHAVIOUR
The expanded universe adds weights to the existing autonomous action scorer. It does not replace movement/pathfinding or furniture action execution.

An action can be influenced by:
- care need urgency
- personality traits
- current mood
- Bond/relationship
- recent actions
- furniture familiarity/favourite status
- routine/time context
- available furniture
- randomness

Traits bias choices; they do not create hard absolute rules.

7. FURNITURE RELATIONSHIPS
Furniture gains an individual relationship record after the dragon notices/uses it. Familiarity progresses from noticed toward familiar/liked based on actual use.

These records support emergent favourites and preference presentation in the Book. Stored favourite objects are not supposed to be forgotten simply because the house is redecorated.

8. HABITS / TRANSITIONS
Recent activity and transitions are bounded. Repeated meaningful sequences can become habits. This is intentionally softer than a fixed schedule: a routine raises probabilities rather than ordering the dragon through a script.

9. RELATIONSHIP
Bond changes trust/comfort, not the dragon's identity. A highly bonded independent dragon remains independent; it simply has a secure relationship with the keeper.

Greeting and interaction flavour can reflect attachment, sociability, confidence, energy and current Bond.

10. JOURNAL PRESENTATION
The keeper journal is divided into four clean sections:
- Nature
- Habits
- Life
- Scrapbook

The design intentionally reads like a premium Velmoran keeper field journal rather than an analytics dashboard. Hidden knowledge is presented as unknown/under observation rather than guessed.

11. MEMORY LIMITS
Recent personality activity and life history are capped. Furniture data is created only for encountered objects. This prevents normal long-term play from generating an endlessly growing save snapshot.

12. PERFORMANCE MODEL
Personality logic is event/decision driven, not frame driven. The patch adds no MutationObserver and no permanent high-frequency personality interval. The existing animation/movement renderer remains responsible for visual motion.

13. ADMIN DEBUG
DragonPersonalityDebug.profile()
Returns the current dragon, breed, axes, assigned/discovered traits, quirks, habits, recent activity and transitions.

DragonPersonalityDebug.explainNextAction()
Returns current action score ordering plus relevant care/recent context. Intended for understanding why the dragon is choosing something.

DragonPersonalityDebug.forceThink()
Forces one decision cycle for testing. Admin-only.

14. IMPORTANT PRESERVATION RULES
Future work should not:
- regenerate personality every load;
- expose all hidden traits instantly;
- convert traits into huge reward/XP advantages;
- make a trait objectively bad;
- turn habits into rigid schedules;
- run personality scoring every animation frame;
- add body-wide MutationObservers for the Book;
- replace existing sleep placement/alignment as part of personality work.
