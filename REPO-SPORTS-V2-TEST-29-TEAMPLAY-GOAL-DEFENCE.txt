REPO SPORTS V2 TEST 29 — TEAMPLAY + GOAL-SIDE DEFENDING

SLIGHTLY MORE TEAMPLAY
- This is deliberately a small change, NOT a return to pass-heavy football.
- With ZERO completed passes in the possession:
  - normal attacks have shot intent reduced to 64% of its previous weight
  - passing + driving receive a small boost
- With ONE completed pass:
  - shot intent is reduced slightly (82% on normal attacks)
  - passing/driving receive a much smaller boost
- Fast counters are affected much less, so genuine transition chances can still
  produce an early shot.
- Once TWO passes have been completed, the existing anti-pass-loop behaviour
  remains intact and the attack becomes progressively more direct.
- Result: more one-two / support involvement before chances, without forcing
  every possession into a long passing sequence.

GOAL-SIDE DEFENDING
- Added a real goal-side screen point calculated BETWEEN the ball carrier and
  the most likely hoop.
- During FINAL THIRD and SHOT SEQUENCE phases, the designated defender is
  reserved as the goal protector.
- Another defender is free to press the carrier from the side/behind.
- The goal protector tracks the shooting lane rather than just hovering near
  the attacker.
- The secondary presser also compresses more strongly goal-side.

BIG CHANCES
- During the visible shot wind-up, the closing defender now targets the lane
  between shooter and hoop, rather than simply flying to the shooter's current
  coordinates.
- Shot wind-up is only very slightly longer to give that physical close-down
  enough time to be visible.

IMPORTANT BUG FIX
- Goal-area congestion previously marked the keeper as an essential goal-action
  participant, BUT could still fan them OUT of the hoop core once they got there.
- Essential carrier/shooter/keeper/receiver/challenger riders are now always
  allowed to remain in the immediate hoop area.
- This directly prevents defenders being artificially pushed away while an
  attacker is sitting on the rings.
