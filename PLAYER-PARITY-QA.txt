REPO SPORTS PLAYER PARITY QA — V22.20.1

ROOT CAUSE CONFIRMED
The previous engine guaranteed TEAM 50/50 fairness, but not PLAYER scoring parity.
Fixed-role attackers had four goal advantages: higher shooting, faster movement, attacker-weighted runner selection, +2.0 percentage-point goal conversion boost, and automatic normal penalties.

PATCH ASSERTIONS
- role-specific goal conversion boost removed
- normal penalty taker no longer hard-coded to attacker
- advanced runner no longer hard-coded/weighted toward attacker
- shooting used for scoring is role-neutral
- movement envelope used for scoring access is role-neutral
- defensive/passing role identity remains
- JavaScript syntax: PASS (node --check)
