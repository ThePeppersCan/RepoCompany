VELMORA / DRAGONBOUND — V33.77
CANTO MEADOW CIRCUIT — PLAYABLE MULTI-TRACK RACING

WHAT'S NEW
- Canto Meadow Circuit is now fully playable from Track Select.
- Uses the supplied Canto Meadow circuit artwork as the actual race background.
- The race spline was traced to the pale circuit itself rather than reusing the City path.
- 6 autonomous racers, 3 laps and 15 ordered checkpoints.
- Existing broadcast systems carry across: camera, race drama, overtakes, final lap, photo finish, shadows/depth, AI pool, official results and Keeper Mark reward presentation.
- Race state/stats are track-specific so Canto keeps its own best time/best lap/race history.

CANTO KEEPER MARK BALANCE
Base reward by finishing position:
  1st  85 Marks
  2nd  68 Marks
  3rd  58 Marks
  4th  50 Marks
  5th  44 Marks
  6th  39 Marks

Average base payout: 57.33 Marks/race.
Estimated at the current ~122 second race cycle: ~1,692 base Marks/hour.
For comparison City Circuit remains ~1,510 base Marks/hour.
This is roughly a 12% progression increase rather than a large economy jump.

Bonuses are unchanged:
- New personal best: +8
- New best lap: +5
- First completed race of the UK day: +10
- First win of the UK day: +10

City Circuit payouts are UNCHANGED.

ACCESS / PROGRESSION
- Canto remains designed as the future Dragon Racing Lv. 9 circuit.
- Dragon Racing XP is still disabled, so Canto currently has PRESEASON ACCESS rather than being impossible to enter.
- Track Details displays: Preseason access · Future Lv. 9.
- When Dragon Racing XP/progression launches later, the normal Lv. 9 gate can be enabled.

TRACK FEEL
- Slightly wider/open camera treatment than City.
- Tiny track-specific pace lift for the broad meadow circuit.
- Wider legal racing corridor to suit the supplied road.
- No manual steering; the race remains fully autonomous.

AUDIO
- No dedicated Canto race music was supplied with this update.
- Canto therefore retains the existing VRN race audio for now rather than inventing/replacing music.

NOT CHANGED
- City Circuit path, payouts or balance.
- Racer RNG model.
- Dragon skill/personality influence.
- Existing race camera logic beyond the per-track zoom multiplier.
- Audio volumes.
- Dragon sprite facing QA / Iskandar V33.76 fix.
- Keeper Mark bonus values.
- Dragon Racing XP remains disabled.
- Bedroom/sleep systems.
- Site performance guard.
