Repo Sports / Dragonbound V32.86 — Needs, Mood & Bond

- Added a live Dragon Care HUD showing Hunger, Hygiene, Energy, Fun and Bond.
- Needs are displayed as high-is-good care bars and update live while the dragon is at home.
- Added a mood label driven by the dragon's current needs (e.g. Content, Hungry, Scruffy, Sleepy, Bored, Lonely).
- Added persistent Hygiene as a new need. Baths, sand baths and grooming now restore it; exercise makes dragons get dirty a little faster.
- Furniture choice is now strongly need-driven. Hungry dragons seek food, dirty dragons seek baths/grooming, tired dragons prefer beds, and bored dragons seek toys/training.
- If a proper bed is available, a tired dragon strongly prefers going to the bed instead of sleeping on the bare floor.
- Existing furniture interactions now have meaningful care effects: food restores Hunger, beds restore Energy, baths restore Hygiene, play/training restores Fun.
- Petting now improves Fun/social comfort and adds Bond.
- Dragon Bites now also improve Hunger/Fun and add a small amount of Bond as each treat is eaten.
- Added gentle offline need progression capped at six hours so dragons continue to feel alive without punishing players for being away.
- Bond never decays in this phase. Full bond-level progression/rewards are reserved for the next relationship update.
- Needs and Bond persist inside the existing Dragonbound server memory save system; no database schema migration is required.
- Admin-only console helper added: DragonboundCareDebug.inspect() / DragonboundCareDebug.set({...}).

Preserves V32.85 Repo Passport slab favourite fix, V32.84 furniture pack, Dragon Bites, house visits, furniture interactions and previous Dragonbound fixes.
