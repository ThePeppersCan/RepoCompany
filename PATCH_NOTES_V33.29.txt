Velmora V33.29 — Travel Map Load Hotfix

CRITICAL FIX
- Removed the whole-document MutationObserver from the V33.28 travel-map patch.
- That observer watched childList while the travel map itself changed childList, allowing a self-triggering loop that could freeze startup.
- Travel-map enhancement now runs only on DOM ready, Dragonbound open, and Travel button open.
- Open/closed pill text now updates only when its value actually changes.
- No day-cycle, bedroom, avatar, pet, or travel-map art/coordinates were changed in this hotfix.
