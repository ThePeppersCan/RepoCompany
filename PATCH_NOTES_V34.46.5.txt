CHANGE
The central room now fits fully inside its existing 677 x 417 panel instead of
being cropped to fill it. Depending on the image proportions, a small dark
margin may remain above/below or beside the room.

The six guest anchors and sprite sizes are mapped from their original 547 x 430
lobby calibration onto the same fitted image scale and origin. This restores
the relationship between characters and sofa when the panel proportions change,
rather than scaling the room and guest positions independently.

Image dimensions are read after load. The mapping updates for image reloads,
resizing and desktop theme activation. The adjustment applies only to the
central greenhouse desktop scene. The previous panel/avatar fixes are retained.

INSTALL
1. Back up index.html and repo-greenhouse-theme.css.
2. Copy all three website files listed above into the website root.
3. Hard-refresh the browser.
