WHAT CHANGED

1. Q70 SUBMIT TERMS PROGRESSION REPAIR
- SUBMIT TERMS now immediately shows a visible Quickquill review/saving state.
- Demand controls are safely disabled while the terms are being recorded.
- A successful save redraws Q70 as the accepted/counter-offer response.
- CONTINUE · LEAVE BOARDROOM advances to Q71.
- Save failures are shown inside The Verdict instead of failing invisibly.
- A network exception can no longer leave storySaving permanently locked.
- Failed submissions keep the player's selected clauses available for retry.

2. CONTRACT-ROOM BUILD-UP
- Q66 now starts with the original Quickquill summons and explains exactly why
  the player returned to HQ.
- The meeting is initially an end-of-assessment review, not an unexplained
  contract appointment.
- Tyrese and Nell establish that Canto, Blackglass and Lumerre will be reviewed
  before Quickquill decides whether there is a future to discuss.
- Q67 is explicitly a ten-minute wait outside the boardroom.
- After three HQ moments, Nell personally calls the player into the room.
- Q68 opens with Mara explaining the assessment-review order.
- Q69 only reveals the contract after the review is complete.

3. COMPATIBILITY
- Q66-Q74 remain the same nine Verdict scenes.
- Existing Chapter Seven saves remain valid.
- Existing contract logic, leverage, Sunscale interest and final decisions are
  preserved.
- No database migration is required.
- V34.30.2 full-screen Verdict viewport isolation remains in place.
