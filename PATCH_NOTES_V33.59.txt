Velmora Dragon Racing — Patch V33.59
===================================

Patch-only update.

What changed
------------
- Reworked sky ambience so it feels cleaner and far more natural.
- Reduced cloud count heavily.
- Clouds now spawn randomly and drift left-to-right more naturally.
- Airships / hot-air balloons now vary in size, height, speed and bobbing instead of moving in rigid static lines.
- Moved the sky traffic into the upper band of the race presentation so it stays out of the way more.
- Switched the sky traffic to proper <img> sprites to avoid the ugly boxed look.
