VELMORA: DRAGONBOUND — V33.80
THE LIVING DRAGON UPDATE

GOAL
Make each dragon feel like an individual pet the keeper gradually gets to know, rather than a breed sprite with a few static stats.

PERSONALITY UNIVERSE
- Adds 133 data-driven personality traits across energy, rhythm, social behaviour, keeper attachment, curiosity, play, food, training, sleep, cleanliness, emotion, courage, environment and routine.
- Adds 51 small quirks that can emerge from observed behaviour.
- Each dragon gets stable deterministic personality axes and a persistent personality universe. It does not reroll on reload/travel/reopen.
- Core/secondary traits are hidden initially and become keeper discoveries from actual evidence.
- Existing Dragonbound traits remain compatible and can coexist with the expanded universe.
- Trait combinations influence autonomous action weighting, furniture interest, greetings, interaction flavour and routine formation without making any personality objectively bad.

LIVING BEHAVIOUR
- Personality now affects WHY the existing baby-dragon AI chooses actions; existing movement/action execution remains in place.
- Adds individual furniture familiarity stages and long-term furniture relationships.
- Favourites emerge from repeated real use rather than being immediately assigned.
- Dragons can form soft dislikes and comforts while keeping essential care usable.
- Recent-action/transitional history helps produce habits such as post-meal naps, bedtime wandering and training/rest routines.
- Mood, bond and personality combine for keeper-facing behaviour.
- Keeper greetings can differ by attachment, independence, shyness, energy and Bond.
- Petting/treat interactions gain small personality-specific reactions.
- Adds sparse in-world emotional expressions/thought fragments; these are cooldown-limited and not constant speech.
- Adds more structured keeper-life memories while keeping history bounded.

DISCOVERY
- Personality is intentionally not dumped into the Book at hatch.
- Evidence is accumulated from actual play/behaviour.
- Strong innate traits generally reveal sooner; secondary nature takes more evidence.
- Quirks also require relevant evidence before becoming known.
- Discovery notifications remain small and Dragonbound-styled.

KEEPER JOURNAL / GRAPHICS
- Reworks My Dragon into a clean four-tab keeper field journal:
  1. Nature — personality, description, discoveries, quirks, relationship and current obsession.
  2. Habits — favourites, routines, comforts, dislikes and daily-life patterns.
  3. Life — growth, training and compact life statistics.
  4. Scrapbook — recent moment and meaningful life history.
- Visual treatment is parchment/field-guide based with muted sage/ink accents, thin rules, subtle stamps and restrained hover/focus states.
- Avoids generic brown game panels and oversized modern dashboard cards.
- Unknown personality information is displayed as something to discover rather than fabricated data.
- Existing responsive/mobile journal behaviour is retained and extended.

PERFORMANCE / SAFETY
- Built on the V33.75 Site Performance Guard version of script.js.
- Does not add any MutationObserver.
- Does not add permanent site-wide polling or a 60 FPS personality simulation.
- Personality decisions hook into existing low-frequency action/interaction transitions.
- No new repeating interval was added by this patch.
- Bounded recent actions, memories and furniture relationship data prevent unbounded save growth.
- Existing V33.75 performance diagnostics remain present.

PRESERVED
- Existing baby-dragon movement and house pathing.
- Furniture collision and Z-order systems.
- Existing needs/care balance and forgiving decay philosophy.
- Existing petting and Dragon Bites economy/cooldowns.
- Training/skill progression and Keeper Mark reward authority.
- Dragon Racing systems and Dragon Racing XP state are untouched by this patch.
- Existing sleep presentation/alignment logic was not intentionally redesigned.
- Existing server-authoritative currency/reward logic remains server-authoritative.
