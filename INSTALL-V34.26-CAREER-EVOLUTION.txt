WHAT THIS UPDATE ADDS
- Persistent, save-specific Career Evolution model.
- Natural sporting progression from rookie → prospect → podium threat → winner → contender → title contender.
- Controlled race competitiveness windows for Canto, Blackglass, Lumerre and later races. These are pace targets / expectations, NOT hard-coded finishing places.
- Durable career records: starts, podiums, wins, poles, best finish, best qualifying, overtakes, positions gained, recent form and first milestones.
- Automatic migration of existing Canto and Blackglass result history into Career Evolution. Existing saves are not reset and victories are never invented.
- Persistent first podium / first pole / first victory detection.
- Six distinct established-racer AI profiles for Jalen, Sofia, Luka, Tyrese, Ren and Maya.
- Organic rivalry data and special Tyrese teammate / competitive-tension tracking.
- Reusable attack and defence battle resolver with real pass/fail/mistake/position-change outcomes.
- Stamina/risk-aware battle choices and race-engine configuration.
- Persistent team-order history: received / obeyed / ignored and future consequences.
- Fame, paddock respect, media reputation, Quickquill trust and pressure tracking.
- World-reaction/fame tiers for future crowd, autograph, media and sponsor behaviour.
- Qualifying progression model.
- Future chapter uniqueness metadata so consecutive chapters can avoid repeating the same primary loop.
- New professional Career Evolution screen from the Career Hub PROFILE button.
- Career Evolution is independent for every save slot.
- CatAsthma tester/admin receives an expandable Career Evolution debug readout only.

RACE ENGINE BRIDGE
The Career iframe now sends a serializable `careerEvolution` configuration with Career story race launches.
It also supports bridge requests for:
- dragonbound-career-evolution-config-request
- dragonbound-career-evolution-battle-request
- dragonbound-career-evolution-team-order

The current Canto / Blackglass parent simulator can safely ignore these new fields. The new Lumerre / future race engine should consume them so live gap-closing, attack windows, defending windows, stamina and team orders use the persistent Career model rather than a predetermined finishing-order animation.

IMPORTANT
- V34.26 does NOT hard-force the player into 2nd or 1st.
- It provides controlled competitiveness windows and a real battle model so future races can naturally move forward with the player's career.
- Existing Canto and Blackglass results remain whatever the player genuinely earned.
- The first career victory remains available as a special future cinematic milestone; this patch does not fabricate one.
