Velmora Dragon Racing — Patch V33.68
====================================

City Circuit Keeper Mark rewards are now live.

Payouts
-------
1st: 75 Keeper Marks
2nd: 60 Keeper Marks
3rd: 52 Keeper Marks
4th: 45 Keeper Marks
5th: 40 Keeper Marks
6th: 35 Keeper Marks

Small bonuses
-------------
- New personal best: +8 (requires a previous server-recorded best)
- New best lap: +5 (requires a previous server-recorded best lap)
- First completed race of the UK day: +10
- First win of the UK day: +10

Implementation
--------------
- Secure Supabase race session starts when City Circuit begins.
- Reward claim is one-time per verified race session.
- Server validates race session age, position and timing ranges before awarding Marks.
- Keeper Marks are credited directly to the existing Dragonbound wallet.
- Results screen shows a compact Keeper Marks reward strip and breakdown.
- Furniture wallet refreshes after reward claim.
- No Dragon Racing XP is awarded.

--------------
- dragon-racing-race.js
- dragon-racing-race.css

Database migration
------------------
- dragonbound_race_keeper_mark_rewards was applied live to Supabase.
