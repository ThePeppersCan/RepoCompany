Velmora Dragonbound V33.36 — Dragon Daily Life V1

Patch-only update.

Implemented:
- Dragon routines now use the existing Velmora in-game clock: morning, active day, evening wind-down and night.
- Personality-weighted personal wake times after 7 AM.
- Stronger evening/night bed seeking and much longer night sleep than daytime naps.
- New occasional keeper-approach behaviour driven by Affection, Bond, Independence and traits, with anti-spam cooldowns.
- Petting now causes a personality-sensitive short reaction/linger instead of immediately returning to random AI.
- Furniture choice now considers familiarity, favourite furniture, favourite categories, curiosity/bravery and time of day.
- New furniture can be investigated gradually rather than treated exactly like known furniture.
- Added persistent observed-behaviour discoveries: Early Riser, Heavy Sleeper, Creature of Habit, Keeper's Shadow, Curious Nose, Quiet Observer, Playful Spark and Evening Nester.
- Added Observed Behaviours to the existing Dragon Care HUD.
- All Daily Life evidence/discoveries/wake scheduling stays inside the existing per-dragon memory save.
- Added admin-only DragonDailyLifeDebug inspect/forceDecision/explainDecision/setRoutinePhase/clearCooldowns/inspectDiscoveries.

Preserved:
- Existing personality/core stats and assigned traits.
- Bond, needs, skills, memories and favourites.
- Existing furniture mounts/collision/dragon sprites.
- Existing petting hitbox and bond-text fixes.
- No database migration required.
