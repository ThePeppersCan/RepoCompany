DRAGONBOUND CAREER MODE V34.12 — MEET THE TEAMS

- The existing Teams hotspot in the Career Hub now opens Meet the Teams.
- Meet the Teams runs full-screen inside the existing Career Mode overlay.
- It is part of the current website: no external host, new tab or standalone
  replacement site is used.
