Comp-Crossing · The Long Way Home

An accidental fall begins one continuous climb through seven layers beneath the village. Nothing chases you. WASD moves, Space jumps, Shift sprints, C or Ctrl crouches/slides, E catches ropes, ledges and powered lines. W pumps a swing; Space releases. Jump from a loaded spring or pressure vent to launch. Watch the gold lift lip and the moving landing before committing. Falls remain physical: steer toward a lower ledge, or fall to the basin and climb again. Refuge ledges let you pause but never save height. Leaving suspends your actual current position, including a fall; RESUME CLIMB continues there. Hold R for 1.5 seconds to start again. Reach the original broken crossing after landing in all seven strata.

Find your line

W + Shift runs at full speed. Jump near the edge and steer smoothly in the air. A matching strafe and mouse turn can build extra momentum.

Earn the bounce

Jump off the approach ledge, travel through the air and strike the marked sloped stone from above. Drops range from short rebounds to deep shaft dives. Aim for the wider sloped face; each impact sends you to a different landing height. Keep moving forward.

Run the wall

Jump alongside a wall marked with three pale lines. Move close to engage automatically. Space kicks you away. For a wall-to-wall transfer, kick with Space and steer towards the opposite wall with A or D, then kick again. The mine and city chain these over open shafts.

Stay low

Tap Ctrl or C while running to slide. An early tap just before landing also works. Hold C or Ctrl to stay low; release to stand when clear. At low speed, either key crouches. Fullscreen enables browser shortcut protection where supported.

Use the rigging

E grabs a nearby rope or cable. Hold W to pump a swing, then Space to release towards the far ledge. On zip lines, ride to the landing or release with Space. The city’s powered cables climb around towers and brake before the landing. Tap Ctrl or C into a mine chute; it carries you through the spiral to the landing. S gently brakes. Mountain launch ramps take a sprint or a jump onto their marked face.

You’re It

Yellow edges mark the route. E grabs a yellow cable; press E again to let go. Space stays jump and will not release these cables. E boards the coaster; jump into the yellow bucket to ride the ball river. The vehicles carry you through and eject you at the catch pads. Ctrl or C locks into a ramp slide; aim for the lit landing after launch. One life in both modes.

Deadbolt

Cross the bedroom, study, bathroom and the furnished rooms downstairs. Chain books, drawers, ramps and rebounds; E grabs cords and the chandelier wire. Ctrl or C slides beneath handles and down the banister. Jump onto the skateboard on the dining table, then keep running, jumping and sliding across its cargo while it rolls through the house. Jump from the nose onto the footstool to dismount. The Caretaker tears up the furniture behind you. Follow the worn edges to the letterbox.

Break a Leg

Follow warm footlights and worn stage marks through the dressing rooms, scenery workshop, auditorium and fly tower. Keep running and jumping across the scenic wagon, counterweight platform and revolving set. E grabs trapezes and fly cables; Ctrl or C enters the fallen-curtain slides. Aim for the angled canvas rebounds. The Director pulls the theatre apart behind you; the storm stays outside the windows.

Overgrowth

Follow pale moss, worn roots and lanterns from daylight through the swamp to the abandoned greenhouse. High routes offer wider catches; low routes are tighter and faster. Hold Ctrl or C before the mouth of mud, hollow-log and petal slides. E grabs hanging vines and planters; W pumps, Space releases. Keep jumping across the sinking skiff and rotating irrigation gallery, then jump to the marked dock. The drowned horde gives way to Mother Bloom at the greenhouse doors. Both songs follow that same handover.

Rupture

Follow worn amber safety paint through the dam. E grabs the crest crane hook and spillway cable; W pumps a swing, Space releases. Land on angled hydraulic plates to redirect your momentum. Burst pipes launch once per run. Hold Ctrl or C at the penstock mouth to enter its curved slide. Keep jumping across the sluice inspection carriage as it descends, then jump to the lit fixed landing.

On the Menu

You dream you are a rat in a midnight kitchen. Myron is the ginger-and-white pursuer; Rivia is the brown tabby. Follow flour arrows through eight districts. At the fork, choose wider boards or narrow cutlery. E grabs ropes, cables and cloth; W pumps, Space releases. C enters the curved slides; A and D bank, S brakes. Land on gold utensil ramps from above. Your normal hands, pistol and knife keep their original animations with rat-styled skin. Allow 8–10 minutes once you know the route. Chain staggered saucers, switchback stairs and narrow cutlery; brake onto the small drop landings. Time the rising lids and overhead shutters, and jump against the conveyor belts. Keep moving across cracked china. On the prep trolley, service trolley and rising rack, follow the route forward and jump to the fixed gold dock.

The Last Draft

Follow the red ink across a world drawn around you. Space jumps, Ctrl or C slides, E grabs a hanging line, W pumps and Space releases. Folded paper and the storm wave are real moving ramps: keep steering and jump normally. Press keys dip under your step. Run through perforated paper doors; keep moving across CRACK-marked sheets. Pale pencil outlines become dark ink before you arrive. The artist grows impatient, but the route is the same every run.

RE-PEAK

Explore the airport and press E at Gate 07 to board. Your timed climb begins at the wreck on the shore through forest, canyon, ice, mist and ruins to the volcanic heart and summit. Ivory markings show dependable landings; gold arrows mark optional expert shortcuts. Pale branches bend under you: press Space for an immediate, steerable spring. Brake early on blue ice and jump from exposed stone. E grabs vines and cables; W pumps, E or Space releases. C enters the banked gullies. Follow the gold drop markers and jump into marked thermal drafts. The Whiteout follows in One Life; Race has no pursuit and a fall restarts your climb while others continue. Allow around 13 minutes for a clean run. Chain wall transfers, spring launches, crumbling shelves and branch releases through distinct biomes.

Castaway

Keep a piece of the night. Pale moonstone edges mark real landings. C enters the curved cloud slides; A and D bank, S brakes. Sprint into the glowing hinged ramps, and steer towards their angled catch. E catches an orbital swing; W pumps and Space releases. Follow the warm rings into the great feather falls. The burning dawn consumes the route behind you in One Life; Race has no pursuit. Allow 7–8 minutes for a clean run.

Text Quest

You are outside a house that has no address. Follow pale edges and lanterns through six chapters to the North Gate. At a command doorway, choose OPEN, CLIMB or DESCEND by taking its physical route. Your movement controls stay the same: E catches a chain, W pumps the swing, Space releases, and Ctrl or C enters a stone channel. A and D bank; S brakes. The Unmentioned watches from a distance, then pursues during three marked passages. Allow around eight minutes for a clean run. The entire world and your hands use a two-colour engraved palette. Complete Normal to earn Errant.

Choose your hold

1 equips bare hands, 2 the unloaded Warden pistol, 3 your selected knife. F cycles its inspect and flourish animations. Choose a model and finish in RUNNERS & KNIVES. Short flourishes continue through ordinary running jumps; ropes, wall runs and slides take priority. Reduced motion disables automatic flourishes. These are cosmetic: all three run and jump identically, with no shooting or damage. Weapons stow automatically when you grab rigging.

Read the world

Lamps show the way. Pale marked edges show solid landings. Jagged red-marked side masonry is unstable and gives way if you land on it. Cracked bridges collapse two seconds after you touch them. R gives you an instant fresh run.

One life on both difficulties. Falls are fatal in chase courses. Comp-Crossing uses physical falls and lost height. No checkpoint respawns, double jump or air dash.
